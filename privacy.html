01

Scope and Privacy Roles

This Policy covers the Bingwa Flash Android app, bingwaflash.co.ke, public agent order pages, client-registration pages, and related support and online services. "Bingwa Flash", "we", "us", and "our" refer to the operator of those services.

Bingwa Flash is the data controller for account, platform, payment-processing, security, diagnostic, and support data that we decide how to use. An agent normally decides how to use customer contacts, offers, messages, and transaction records for that agent's business and is responsible for giving customers any notice required for that use.

Bingwa Flash is an independent automation platform. It is not Safaricom PLC, M-Pesa, a bank, or a mobile network operator, and those services remain controlled by their respective providers.

02

Data We Handle

Depending on the features used, we may handle:

  • Account and public profile data: email, agent and business names, username, phone numbers, Till or PayBill details, verification status, registration dates, and active-device/session information.
  • Configuration: offers, prices, payment modes, SIM choices, USSD mappings, retry and verifier rules, schedules, reply templates, alert settings, Relay rules, Portal settings, security preferences, and web-order settings.
  • Transaction and client data: M-Pesa reference, customer name or phone where available, amount, selected offer, status, timestamps, carrier responses, recovery history, commission, blacklist state, and client totals.
  • Payment and entitlement data: token or Time Token balance, package, payment phone, checkout identifiers, payment result, subscription period where applicable, payout destination, and reconciliation records.
  • Technical and support data: app version, device model or label, operating-system information, app-instance or advertising identifiers made available by Android or Google services, crash and performance diagnostics, security events, request metadata, support correspondence, and information you choose to send with a report.

We do not ask for or store an M-Pesa PIN. Security PINs are protected using one-way hashes; a temporary support-issued PIN is shown to the administrator for handoff but is not stored as readable PIN text.

03

Device Permissions and On-Device Processing

Android permissions are requested to make a selected feature work. The app may read supported SMS senders to detect payment confirmations, carrier responses, Relay packets, verifier messages, and recovery signals; send SMS for configured customer replies, Relay, and agent alerts; place USSD calls on the chosen SIM; and keep active automation running in the background.

Accessibility is used during enabled Advanced automation to read the active carrier USSD window, enter the saved steps, capture the response, and close or recover the carrier dialog. Overlay, notification, wake, alarm, battery-optimisation, and phone-state access support visibility and durable queue recovery. Contacts permission supports contact search, client import, and an agent's optional save-to-contacts action. Camera access scans pairing QR codes. Bluetooth access connects enabled Relay devices. The app-update installer permission is used only when the agent chooses to install an APK update.

The app does not request location permission. Android may still associate older Bluetooth APIs with nearby-device behaviour. Denying an optional permission disables the related feature; it does not give Bingwa Flash permission to use the data for unrelated advertising.

Raw SMS, local contacts, USSD responses, and transaction history are processed primarily on the agent phone. They leave the device only when an enabled feature requires transmission, such as an SMS reply, online Relay, Portal sync, web order, support report, or cloud account sync.

04

Local Storage and Cloud Sync

Full transaction history and the main client list are stored primarily on the agent's device. Local records remain until the agent deletes them, runs a cleanup, clears app data, or uninstalls the app. Device contact records are controlled by the selected contacts provider and may separately sync through that provider.

Agent account and configuration fields are also saved locally for offline operation and mirrored to the agent profile online. This allows account recovery, active-device control, settings continuity, web-order publishing, verification, tokens, security reset, and other connected features. Not every local transaction is uploaded by default.

Google Analytics may receive standard app/session events, device information, and an app-instance or advertising identifier where Android and the user's device settings make it available. Firebase Crashlytics may receive crash traces, app/device context, and diagnostic keys. Bingwa Flash does not contain an advertising network or use this information to serve targeted advertisements inside the app.

05

Public Pages, Orders, and Payments

If enabled by an agent, a public order page may display the agent's username, agent or business name, business phone, Till number, verification status, availability, and selected offers. Client registration may collect a name, phone number, selected agent, and a protected registration PIN.

For a web order or paid Bingwa Flash feature, we process the paying phone, receiving phone, offer or package, amount, M-Pesa reference, checkout identifiers, status, timestamps, and payout or reconciliation details. Payment requests and payouts are carried by M-Pesa/Safaricom infrastructure under their terms. We do not receive the customer's M-Pesa PIN.

Order and payout data may be retained after completion where reasonably needed for accounting, reconciliation, fraud prevention, chargeback or reversal handling, customer support, legal claims, and regulatory obligations.

06

Relay and Portal Features

Relay can transfer a transaction packet by SMS, Online, Hotspot/Wi-Fi, or Bluetooth. A packet may contain a stable transaction identity, payment reference, customer or receiver details, amount, sender identity, and authentication information. SMS uses the mobile network; Hotspot and Bluetooth communicate between nearby devices; Online Relay temporarily stages encrypted-in-transit packet data in cloud storage until the receiving workflow claims it.

Pair codes authenticate compatible Relay packets but are not a substitute for device security. Agents should share QR and manual connection details only with an intended partner and remove rules that are no longer trusted.

When Portal is enabled, selected device readiness, balances, offer data, transaction summaries/details, and authorised commands may be mirrored through the agent's cloud account so linked sessions can display or control supported functions. Turning Portal off stops new Portal sync, but existing security, audit, or transaction records may remain for the retention periods below.

07

Purposes and Legal Bases

We process data only where needed to perform our agreement with a user, follow the user's settings or consent, comply with law, or pursue legitimate interests that are not overridden by a person's rights. Those purposes include:

  • creating and protecting accounts, sessions, PIN recovery, verification, and fraud controls;
  • matching payments, running configured automation, recovering queues, sending selected replies or alerts, and showing auditable results;
  • publishing enabled offers, processing web orders and paid features, reconciling payment results, and arranging payouts;
  • operating Relay, Portal, client registration, support, app updates, analytics, crash diagnosis, and security monitoring;
  • enforcing our Terms, responding to disputes, keeping required records, and protecting users, the service, and the public.
08

Sharing, Processors, and International Transfers

We do not sell or rent personal data. We disclose only what is reasonably necessary:

  • to Google/Firebase for authentication, cloud database, hosting, functions, analytics, and crash diagnostics;
  • to Safaricom/M-Pesa and relevant payment partners to initiate, confirm, reconcile, pay out, or investigate a transaction;
  • to an agent and that agent's authorised linked devices or Relay partner for a request the customer or agent initiated;
  • to professional advisers, contractors, or support providers under confidentiality and purpose restrictions;
  • to regulators, courts, law enforcement, or affected parties where law requires it or where reasonably necessary to investigate fraud, security incidents, abuse, or protect legal rights.

Cloud providers may process data outside Kenya. Where applicable, we rely on contractual, technical, organisational, consent, or other lawful safeguards required for cross-border processing. Third-party services also apply their own privacy terms.

09

Retention

We keep data only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose described in this Policy, subject to backups, dispute holds, fraud prevention, accounting, and legal requirements. In general:

  • local app history remains until the agent deletes it, clears app data, or uninstalls;
  • account and active configuration data remains while the account is open and for a limited period afterward;
  • temporary online client-registration and Relay inbox payloads are removed after successful claim where the workflow permits;
  • completed payment, payout, security, fraud, and order records may be retained longer to reconcile funds, answer disputes, enforce rights, and comply with law;
  • crash, analytics, hosting, and security logs follow the retention controls of the relevant service and our configured operational needs.

An account-deletion request removes or anonymises data we control unless retention is necessary for a pending transaction, security investigation, legal claim, payment record, or legal obligation. Deletion from Bingwa Flash does not remove copies independently held by an agent, the user's phone, contacts provider, mobile operator, payment provider, or a recipient.

10

Security and Incident Handling

We use access controls, authenticated sessions, encrypted network connections, database rules, one-way PIN hashing, encrypted local security storage, app signing, bounded listeners, duplicate protection, and operational monitoring appropriate to the service. Sensitive Relay authentication values and transaction content are excluded from diagnostic logs by design.

No application, phone, mobile network, or cloud service is completely secure. Agents must keep Android and Bingwa Flash updated, protect the phone and SIM, use a unique PIN, restrict linked devices, review public information, and report suspected compromise promptly. Where a personal-data breach creates a legal notification duty, we will investigate and notify affected persons or the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as required.

11

Your Rights and Choices

Subject to the Kenya Data Protection Act and applicable exceptions, a person may ask to be informed about processing, access data we control, object or withdraw consent, correct inaccurate data, request deletion, or request restriction or portability where available. We may need to verify identity and may retain a record of the request.

  • Agents can turn off automation, individual Relay rules, Portal, public web orders, website registration, alerts, auto-reply, and contact saving.
  • Agents can delete supported local client and transaction records, export selected data where the app provides that action, or clear app data through Android.
  • Customers should first contact the agent for records that agent controls. They may use the website removal flow where available.
  • For Bingwa Flash-controlled account or platform data, email support@bingwaflash.co.ke with the account email or phone and the request. Do not send an M-Pesa PIN, security PIN, or unredacted secrets.
12

Children, Automated Processing, and Policy Changes

Bingwa Flash is a business automation service intended for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly invite children to open agent accounts or purchase paid features. If a minor's information appears in an agent's customer records, the agent remains responsible for having a lawful basis and any required guardian involvement.

The app automatically matches configured payment signals to offers and may queue, execute, retry, hold, or fail a transaction according to agent rules. Agents can review transaction details and use available manual actions. We do not use customer data for credit scoring or solely automated decisions that produce legal effects.

We may update this Policy when features, providers, security practices, or law change. Material changes will be identified by a new effective date and may also be announced in the app or website. Continued use after the effective date is subject to the updated Policy, without limiting rights that require separate consent.

13

Contact and Complaints

For privacy questions, rights requests, account deletion, or a suspected incident, email support@bingwaflash.co.ke. Include enough information to identify the relevant account or order, but redact PINs and unrelated message content.

If a privacy concern is not resolved, a person may contact or lodge a complaint with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner. This does not limit any other remedy available under applicable law.
