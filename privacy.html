01

Scope and privacy roles

This Policy covers the Bingwa Flash Android app, bingwaflash.co.ke, SiteLink order and client-registration pages, Bingwa Flash Portal, Bridge, the official download site, and related payment, account and support services. Bingwa Flash is operated by Lyco Technologies. Contact us at support@bingwaflash.co.ke.

We do not sell or rent personal data. We use it to provide, protect and support Bingwa Flash. The disclosures below explain when agents, service providers and others receive it.

Bingwa Flash decides how to use data for platform accounts, access, payments, security and support. Agents decide how to run their businesses and how to use customer contacts, offers, transaction records and messages. They must give customers the notices and choices required for that use. We process data on an agent's instructions where a feature handles their business records on their behalf. Mobile networks and payment providers apply their own privacy terms.

02

Account, profile and device data

  • Sign-in and account records. Email, account ID, sign-in provider and verification state, registration dates, profile names, website username, sign-in and session history, linked devices, security settings and changes to account email or password. Google Sign-In may provide the account identity and profile information you authorise. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in credentials.
  • Business and payment settings. Agent and business names, optional profile photo, Customer contact, the separate Agent phone and Agent Alerts number, Till or Paybill details, Send Money number, payout destination, SiteLink delivery number, and verification or subscription status. The private Agent phone and the customer-facing contact have different purposes.
  • Device and network records. Android or installation identifiers, device labels or model, app and OS version, active device and session information, Firebase push token and notification state, request IP address and timestamps, and security or abuse-prevention records. We may keep up to 20 changes to the account's observed public IP address for troubleshooting and security. An IP address may describe a network shared by many people.
  • Configuration and administration. Offers, prices, SIM choices, USSD templates and learned steps, retry and verifier rules, schedules, reply and alert templates, Bridge and Portal settings, notification delivery and acknowledgment, and records of administrator actions. Where profile history is available, authorised Panel administrators can refer to up to ten previous values per supported field with the dates they were replaced. That feature records future changes from activation.

We never ask you to send us your M-PESA PIN. The app's Security PIN uses a one-way verifier. Android handles biometric authentication when enabled. The SiteLink client-registration PIN is a separate code. Use a unique code and never reuse a banking or device PIN.

03

Android permissions and background operation

  • SMS. The app can receive and read SMS to identify supported payment, carrier, verifier, reversal and Bridge messages. When you use inbox search or recovery, the app can inspect stored messages on the device. It can send replies, alerts, Bridge packets, shop responses and Bulk SMS or Marketer messages from the selected SIM. Bulk SMS may ask you to make Bingwa Flash the default SMS app temporarily. Android lets you change the default again.
  • Accessibility and phone. With permission, the app reads relevant carrier windows and their text, enters saved choices, captures responses and closes or recovers USSD dialogs. This supports visible and interactive USSD, Quick Dial and Mapping Learning. Phone and SIM access lets the app choose a subscription and place authorised USSD requests or configured calls.
  • Contacts, camera and nearby devices. Contacts access supports lookup, import and saving selected contacts. The camera can scan Bridge pairing QR codes. Nearby Bluetooth and Wi-Fi or hotspot access supports Bridge pairing and transport. Android 11 and older may require location permission for Bluetooth discovery. That permission supports discovery.
  • Other Android controls. Notifications, overlays, wake and vibration, exact alarms, boot and background operation, and battery settings support the queue, alerts, recovery and scheduled work. Android may require shared storage permission on older devices for exports, and permission to install packages when you choose an APK update.

You can manage permissions and default apps in Android Settings. Turning off a permission can stop the features that depend on it. Turning off automation may still leave SMS intake and records, recovery, or separately enabled connectivity available.

04

Local records, offers and exports

Routine SMS intake, transaction history and the main Client Management list are stored primarily on the agent's device. Records may include payment references and messages, names and phone numbers, sending and receiving details, amounts, purchase or debt history, points, streaks, referrals, blocked status, timestamps, selected offers, SIM, carrier responses, verification evidence, reversals, and recovery decisions. The app uses these for automation, queue review, M-PESA Messages, customer management, statistics, commission and airtime or Bonga checks.

Some profile and offer settings sync for account recovery and connected features. Portal, SiteLink, Internet Bridge, payment processing, alerts and user-requested sharing can send the information described in this Policy outside the phone. Android system backup is disabled for the app. Records that exist only on a phone may be lost if you clear app data or uninstall. Contacts saved to Android can sync through the chosen contacts account.

Agents can import contacts or CSV/VCF files. A client CSV can contain names, phones, spending, points and active or blocked status. A vCard contains names and phones. Offer files and Share Links can contain the selected commercial settings and USSD templates. A Share Link can record the creator, authorised importer, times and use counts. Copying or exporting records, offers, messages, receipts or logs gives the chosen app or recipient a separate copy.

05

SiteLink, client registration and payments

When an agent publishes SiteLink, visitors can see the page's business identity, username, profile photo if provided, customer contact and payment information, listed offers, prices, availability and verification badge. Published profiles, photos and share previews can be cached or indexed by browsers, search engines and messaging services. Some legacy online records are accessible without signing in. This broader access can include agent account and contact information, offer settings, and client registration details and codes beyond the fields displayed on the page.

To register with an agent, the client can provide a name, phone number and a registration PIN. We use the linked registration and its status to deliver it to the agent and handle removal requests. A SiteLink removal request affects the linked registration. A copy already imported to the agent's device, a reusable registration PIN and separate payment records may remain.

For a SiteLink order or purchase of an app feature, we handle the selected agent, package, paying and receiving phones where applicable, amount, checkout and receipt identifiers, status and timestamps, payment confirmation, delivery handoff, payout and reconciliation records. The chosen agent and Safaricom/M-PESA receive the information needed for their roles. SiteLink can deliver an order through an internet connection to the agent's phone or by coded SMS, according to the agent's selected mode. Payment confirmation, phone receipt, payout and fulfilment each have their own status.

Checkout may keep a limited order-status reference in the browser tab so you can return to the status view. Browser preferences and sign-in state support the site and Portal. Hosting and security providers can receive request data such as IP addresses, browser or device information and access times.

06

Bridge and Portal

Bridge forwards eligible payment information to a processing device chosen by the agent. The signed packet can include the payment reference, amount, customer name and number, payment mode and time, and sender and receiver device or account identifiers. SMS uses the carrier network. Bluetooth and Wi-Fi or hotspot communicate with the nearby partner. Internet Bridge uses cloud staging and authenticated delivery. A recipient can keep its own transaction record. Treat pairing codes and invitations as sensitive.

With Portal sync enabled, linked accounts or devices and authorised operators can view data and issue supported commands within their access. The data can include device label and readiness, last-seen time, network and battery state, SIM and automation settings, offers and mapping, airtime and token balances, schedules, commission and selected customer or transaction records. A transaction view may include customer names and numbers, payment messages and carrier responses. Commands and their results can record the operator, target device, inputs, timestamps and outcome.

Portal can run actions you choose, including retry or reschedule, alternate number or offer, Quick Dial, selected SMS, and settings changes. Turn off sync or remove access when a viewer or device no longer needs it. A device can finish work it already accepted. Previous command, payment and audit records or copies downloaded by viewers may remain.

07

Customer messaging, loyalty and marketing

Agents choose which customer replies, shop menus, Bulk SMS messages and alerts the app sends. The app uses the numbers, templates, transaction details, delivery results and other information needed for those choices. Shop and loyalty features may store points, rewards, purchase streaks, referral activity, redemptions, and any related offer and purchase history.

Marketer can select recipients from Client Management based on recent verified purchases, use configured strategies and timing, and record sending activity and opt-outs. Preview lets the agent inspect proposed messages. Agents must obtain the express permission required for direct marketing, use data lawfully, and offer an accessible way to stop messages. A purchase or appearance in the app's eligible audience does not establish permission.

To stop an agent's Marketer SMS, reply STOP or contact that agent using the details provided. Opt-out records may be kept to honour the request. Account, payment and service messages serve separate purposes.

08

Purposes and legal bases

We use account, payment and service data to provide the features you request, operate checkouts and subscriptions, support users, and keep a record of authorised actions. We use security and operational data where reasonably necessary to protect accounts, diagnose failures, prevent fraud or duplicate payments, and operate the service, taking account of the person's rights. We retain or disclose relevant records where law requires it, including for accounting, disputes and investigations.

Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it for future processing. This Policy explains how data is used. Accepting the Terms, enabling a feature, or placing an order does not supply consent for unrelated uses.

09

Sharing, providers and international transfers

  • Google and Firebase provide sign-in, database, functions, Portal hosting, messaging, app integrity checks, analytics and crash reporting. Firebase Authentication processes data in the United States. Other services may use data centres in South Africa, the United States and elsewhere.
  • Cloudflare serves public website traffic and profile images. When you upload a photo, the app sends a cropped copy for the profile image. The original gallery file stays on your device. GitHub hosts the official download page or APK releases. Public pages also load Google Fonts. Optional links or videos can take you to the relevant external service.
  • Safaricom/M-PESA and other mobile operators process requests and data for SMS, calls, USSD, mobile-money payment, airtime and payouts as applicable.
  • People and organisations. Agents, their chosen Bridge partners and linked Portal viewers, authorised Panel administrators, support providers and advisers can receive information needed for their roles. We may disclose relevant records to courts, regulators, law enforcement or affected parties where required or otherwise permitted by law.

We use safeguards required by applicable law for transfers outside Kenya. Service providers may process operational data under their own terms. Firebase privacy information · Cloudflare privacy information.

10

Retention, local cleanup and account deletion

  • Phone and browser. Local history stays until the user deletes or cleans it up, clears app data or uninstalls. Exports and other recipients' copies have their own lifecycle. Browser caches and session data have separate expiry or clear controls.
  • Accounts and operations. We retain active account, settings and access records while needed to operate the account. Pending Bridge data can remain for recovery. After intake, supported Online Bridge flows remove the packet payload and keep a receipt for duplicate protection. Expired checkout status links and pairing codes are separate from financial, security or operation records.
  • Payments and tokens. We retain purchase, token and subscription grants, transfers, receipts, backup lifecycle, payout and reconciliation data as needed for operations, accounting, disputes, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Token backup records may include the units or time reserved, source and destination devices, creation, expiry, attempts and redemption status.
  • Diagnostics. Share Logs is local and keeps up to 48 hours of events, subject to size and rotation. Analytics, crash reporting, hosting, support and security records follow the relevant service settings and the time reasonably needed for their purposes.

To request account deletion, email support@bingwaflash.co.ke with your account email and a request to delete the Bingwa Flash account. We may verify account ownership and explain any records we must retain. We delete or de-identify data we control when its purpose ends, subject to lawful retention for pending transactions, fraud, disputes and legal requirements. Account deletion does not erase contacts, device exports, carrier records or copies independently held by agents and other recipients.

11

Security, analytics and diagnostics

We use measures such as encrypted internet connections, device and session checks, access rules, scoped administrator tools and protected storage for certain secrets. Separate privacy controls cover such features as the Agent phone, IP history and app Security PIN. No method can guarantee security. Report suspicious access promptly. We will investigate suspected breaches and give notices required by law.

Google Analytics for Firebase can receive app usage, device and app-instance information and advertising identifiers where available and permitted by device and SDK settings. We use app analytics for service operation and improvement. Bingwa Flash does not serve targeted advertising in the app. Firebase Crashlytics can receive crash or app-not-responding reports, stack traces, device or app context and diagnostic events.

Share Logs is separate from automatic crash reporting. It creates a filtered diagnostic ZIP on the phone and lets you save it to Downloads or choose a recipient. The export filters direct identifiers, transaction text and secrets from its event stream. If you contact support, we receive the information and attachments you choose to send. Leave out PINs, passwords, backup codes and unrelated customer data.

12

Your rights and choices

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act and applicable exceptions, you may ask how your information is used, request access or correction, object to processing, withdraw consent, and request restriction, erasure or portability where applicable. You may ask for human review of a decision covered by the Act's automated decision safeguards. We may need to verify who you are.

Agents can change settings, disable Portal or Bridge, unpublish SiteLink, remove links, stop marketing or other messaging, manage contacts and records, and revoke Android permissions. Customers can contact their agent for records or promotions that agent controls. Use the account deletion instructions for a Bingwa Flash account or email us about other data we control.

13

Children, automated processing and changes

Agent accounts are intended for adults. If an agent handles a child's information, the agent is responsible for meeting the applicable legal requirements. Tell us if you believe a child's data was submitted unlawfully.

Saved rules can match payments to offers, select recipients, queue or hold work, control access, and send selected messages. Agents can review outcomes and use supported manual actions. Contact support if you want to challenge a platform decision that affects you.

We may update this Policy as features, providers or law change. We will identify material changes and request separate consent where required.

14

Contact and complaints

For privacy, account deletion or incident reports, email support@bingwaflash.co.ke. You can lodge a complaint with Kenya's Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.
