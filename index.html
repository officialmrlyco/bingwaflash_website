Before you start

Questions agents ask before they trust a workflow.

Short answers for the decisions that should be clear before you connect a payment to an automated carrier action.

Does Bingwa Flash run every payment automatically?

No. A payment needs to meet the offer and payment conditions you configured. Holds, failures, disabled offers, and mapping concerns remain visible instead of being treated as a successful sale.

What happens when Safaricom changes a USSD menu?

Saved mappings and live responses are checked during the flow. A change that does not match the expected route is a reason to stop and review, not a reason to keep sending inputs.

Can I still handle a customer manually?

Yes. The app is designed to give you a controlled record and execution lane, not to remove the agent’s judgement from a customer situation.

Where do I get the Android installer and tutorials?

The installer, tutorial library, WhatsApp, Telegram, and download support live at the official download site so the marketing and ordering site remains focused.
