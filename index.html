How It Runs

One lane for every step that spends a customer’s payment.

Automation should be deliberate. Bingwa Flash verifies conditions, executes USSD interactions sequentially, and leaves an audit trail for every completed sale.

01

Read the payment

Customer payment SMS (Till or PayBill) is captured instantly and matched against your enabled offer amounts.

02

Run the saved flow

Bingwa Flash follows your pre-configured USSD route on the selected SIM card without manual dialing delay.

03

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about USSD automation and safety.

Does Bingwa Flash run every payment automatically?

Yes, any incoming payment that matches an enabled offer amount and configured mode is queued and executed immediately on your selected SIM.

What happens when Safaricom or Airtel changes a USSD menu?

The app detects unexpected carrier dialogs and alerts you immediately so you can adjust your USSD mapping without losing transaction records.

Can I still dial or handle transactions manually?

Yes. You can use Quick Dial for manual numbers or pause automation at any time with a single tap on the dashboard toggle.

Is my data and PIN stored safely?

All transaction records and configuration stay strictly on your device in encrypted local storage. No sensitive carrier PINs are exposed.
