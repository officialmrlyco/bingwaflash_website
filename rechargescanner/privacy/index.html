01

What this policy covers

Airtime Recharge Scanner ("the App") is developed and published by Bingwa Flash. It helps you scan airtime voucher digits, review them, and run a confirmed USSD recharge on your chosen SIM.

Voucher scanning, history, carrier settings, and recharge preparation are handled locally. The App also uses Google Mobile Ads and Google ML Kit, which process limited technical data as described in section 06.

The short version

Bingwa Flash does not receive your voucher digits, recharge history, SIM details, carrier settings, or camera images. Google services used for ads and text recognition may process identifiers, app interactions, approximate location derived from an IP address, and diagnostics.

02

Camera and image use

The App requests camera permission only to read digits from airtime voucher cards and printed voucher lists. Camera access is active while the scanner screen is open. The App does not take photographs for a gallery, record video, use facial recognition, or access the camera in the background.

Camera frames and imported voucher images are processed on-device for text recognition and are immediately discarded after processing. Google states that ML Kit does not send the input image or recognized output to its servers.

  • Camera access is tied to the visible scanning screen.
  • Voucher images are not saved by the scanner.
  • Images and recognized voucher text are not uploaded to Bingwa Flash or Google.
03

Data used by the App

The App does not require an account and does not ask you to provide Bingwa Flash with your name, email address, or phone number. The following operational information is stored locally:

  • Voucher digits you scan or enter and their local history status.
  • Recharge and balance responses returned by your mobile carrier.
  • SIM preferences, editable USSD templates, and carrier settings.
  • Theme choices and rewarded bulk-run credits.

Google SDKs may automatically process Android advertising ID, App Set ID or another per-installation identifier, IP address and approximate location, app launches and interactions, SDK events, device and app information, performance metrics, and diagnostic or error information. Their purposes include advertising, analytics, service performance, and fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

04

Storage, retention, and deletion

The App uses Android's private local storage for voucher history and settings. This information is not remotely accessible to Bingwa Flash. Android may include local app data in a user-initiated device-to-device transfer when you move to a new phone; the App excludes voucher records and settings from Android cloud backup.

You can delete locally stored data through Android Settings > Apps > Airtime Recharge Scanner > Storage > Clear Data, or by uninstalling the App. Data processed by Google is retained and deleted according to Google's policies and the privacy controls available in your Google and Android settings.

05

USSD and carrier actions

When you choose to recharge, the App constructs a USSD string from the reviewed voucher digits and the configured carrier template. It then sends that request through the SIM you selected, in the same way as manually dialing the carrier code.

The App shows a confirmation step before running a recharge. You can edit, save, cancel, or continue. Carrier charges and recharge outcomes are governed by your mobile network provider.

Your decision controls execution

A detected voucher is not automatically submitted. The scan pauses for review before any recharge request is made.

06

Google services

The App uses three Google services with distinct roles:

Google Mobile Ads

Displays banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Google documents collection and sharing of IP address information, app interactions, diagnostics, and device or account identifiers for ads, analytics, and fraud prevention.

Google ML Kit

Runs on-device voucher text recognition. It may send device and app information, per-installation identifiers, performance metrics, feature events, and errors to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics.

Google Play

Distributes the App and provides platform services. Google's terms and privacy practices apply to downloading and installing the App.

Google Mobile Ads protects transmitted data using TLS, and ML Kit uses HTTPS. Read the current Google Mobile Ads disclosure, ML Kit disclosure, and Google Privacy Policy.

The App does not include Bingwa Flash-operated analytics, crash reporting, social media, account, or remote configuration services.

07

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. Bingwa Flash does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App. If you believe a child has used the App in a way that raises a privacy concern, contact us so we can review it.
